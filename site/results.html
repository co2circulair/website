Results

A year and a half of operation, on public record.

The SMART-DAC pilot was built and operated at B9 Energy near Larne, Northern Ireland, from late 2023 to early 2025, in a programme funded by the UK Department for Energy Security and Net Zero (DESNZ). Read the DESNZ final report.

  • 1.5 yearsin operation
  • 25 tonnesCO2 captured
  • 80%of the time in operation, even during storm

The membranes came through Storm Darragh in December 2024, with wind speeds above 70 miles per hour, without loss of performance or damage.

The plant operated reliably, restarted automatically after shutdowns and kept operating through frost.

What comes next

A demonstration installation with a first customer, large enough to deliver CO2 into a real fuel chain.

Vragen aan Berno: mag de omvang van de demonstratie erbij (je noemde 1,5 kiloton per jaar)? Bedragen zetten we er niet op. En het energiegebruik per kilo laten we weg tot het verschil met het DESNZ-rapport is uitgelegd.
