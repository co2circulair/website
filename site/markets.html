Markets

Every fuel made from electricity needs carbon.

Power-to-X producers turn renewable electricity and hydrogen into fuels such as e-methanol. Each of them needs a carbon source. SMART-DAC supplies that carbon from the air, on the site where the fuel is made.

Why carbon from the air

Under European rules, CO2 captured at power stations stops counting towards renewable fuels after 2036, and CO2 from other fossil sources after 2041. What remains is biogenic CO2 or CO2 taken from the air. CO2 from the air has no such end date.

Where SMART-DAC fits

E-methanol and other power-to-X fuels

Our main market. Producers of e-methanol, and of other fuels made from electricity, need a steady carbon stream next to their hydrogen. SMART-DAC delivers that stream from the air, on their own site, with no CO2 transport in between.

Small and local fuel chains

A complete chain that fits on one site: wind turbines for the electricity, an electrolyser for the hydrogen, SMART-DAC for the carbon and a synthesis unit for the methanol. Suited to inland shipping, fishing ports, or a cooperative of farmers whose wind turbines produce electricity at night that they cannot sell.

Greenhouses as a test route

Greenhouses use CO2 every day. A test installation at a greenhouse can show the technology at work, in measured operation, before a first fuel chain is built.
