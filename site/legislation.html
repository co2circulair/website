Framework

DAC related legislation in the European Union

The EU recognises DAC as a necessary but still immature part of its climate policy. The building blocks (certification, storage targets, strategy) are in place. The decisive step, structural funding through the EU ETS or a separate removal target, is still subject to political decision-making.

Jouw vraag 3: blijft DAC-CO2 onder de RFNBO-regels op lange termijn toegestaan, en geeft dat nu al een echt voordeel?

  • In forceEuropeFuelsCheck

    CO2 from the air stays eligible for renewable fuels

    Under the EU rules for renewable fuels of non-biological origin (RFNBOs) and low-carbon fuels, CO2 captured from the air counts as a carbon source without an end date. CO2 from fossil industry does have an end date.

    Source: Delegated Regulations (EU) 2023/1185 and 2025/2359, via CO2 Value Europe

EU ETS and permanence

Using CO2 or storing it.

[Text]

Jouw vraag 5: hoe behandelt het EU ETS CO2 in e-fuels tegenover CO2 die permanent gebonden is?

  • ProposalEuropeAll usesCheck

    Zero emission factor for CO2 from the air in the EU ETS

    The proposal for the revision of the EU Emissions Trading System (ETS) of 17 July 2026 gives CO2 captured from the atmosphere an emission factor of zero (recital 81).

    Source: European Commission proposal, 17 July 2026, via CO2 Value Europe

  • In forceEuropeStorageChemicals & materials

    Removal certificates only for permanent storage

    Under the EU Carbon Removal Certification Framework (CRCF), certificates apply to permanent removals, such as underground storage or binding in building materials. CO2 used in greenhouses or fuels does not earn removal certificates.

    Source: European Commission, 3 February 2026

Benelux

What applies in the Netherlands, Belgium and Luxembourg.

Vraag aan Berno: hoort de Benelux op deze pagina? De kop zegt "EU legislation". Zo niet, dan gaat dit blok weg en komen de Benelux-punten alleen bij het nieuws.

  • In forceBeneluxGreenhousesStorage

    The Netherlands subsidises CO2 captured from the air

    The Dutch SDE++ operating subsidy has a category for CO2 captured from the air and used for CO2 fertilisation in greenhouses. From the 2026 round there is also a category for CO2 from the air that is stored permanently.

    Source: Staatscourant 2026, 25954, articles 79 and 85

    SDE++ 2026 published, with a category for storage of CO2 from the air

    The Dutch government published the rules for the 2026 round. Besides greenhouse use, CO2 captured from the air can now also be subsidised when it is stored permanently.

    Source: Staatscourant 2026, 25954

    ProposalEuropeAll usesCheck

    Commission proposes zero emission factor for CO2 from the air

    In its proposal for the revision of the EU ETS, the European Commission treats CO2 captured from the atmosphere the same as sustainable biomass: an emission factor of zero.

    Source: European Commission proposal, recital 81, via CO2 Value Europe
